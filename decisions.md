One row per decision, newest first. **Decided** rows override older text
anywhere in this repository; fix the older text when you touch it. **Proposed**
rows wait for acceptance. **Open** rows need an answer and an owner.
**Replaced** proposals are kept so old references still resolve.

IDs: `D-<day>.<n>` for October, `D-<day><month>.<n>` from November (D-2Nov.1 is
the first decision of 2 November). D1–D5 are the 22 September proposals.
